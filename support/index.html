Common questions

How do I log something?

Tap the ✨ button on any item. That's it. To add a new item, tap +.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Try:

  • "Log watered plants in When Did I Last"
  • "When did I last water plants in When Did I Last"
  • "Show all activities in When Did I Last"
What's free and what's Pro?

Free lets you track up to 3 active items at a time, with unlimited archived items. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited items, full history and all color schemes. There's no subscription.

I bought Pro, but it isn't unlocked.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

In Settings, use Export Data to save a backup file, then use Import Data on the new phone. Importing replaces everything on that phone. On the free tier, imported items arrive in your Archive, and you can unarchive up to 3.

I travelled. Will my dates be wrong?

No. Date-only items keep the day you logged them, and items with a time show that moment in your current local time. You never have to think about time zones.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
